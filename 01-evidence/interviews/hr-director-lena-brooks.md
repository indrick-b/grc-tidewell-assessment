# Interview Notes — Lena Brooks, HR Director

**Date:** October 28, 2026 · **Interviewer:** GRC Analyst · **Length:** 35 minutes
**Purpose:** Understand onboarding, offboarding, background checks, and security training.

> Fictional interview for a practice assessment.

---

**Q: Walk me through what happens when someone leaves.**
"I process the paperwork, collect the badge and laptop, and send IT a ticket. When it's a sudden termination, I sometimes forget the ticket because there's a lot going on that day. I don't have a checklist, it's mostly from memory."

**Q: Do you confirm IT actually disabled the account?**
"No. I assume they did once I send the ticket."

**Q: Are all laptops returned?**
"Almost always. Jason Mercer in Operations was let go in August and never brought his laptop back. I sent him two emails. I should probably follow up again."

**Q: Do new hires get background checks?**
"Yes, every new hire since 2018 gets a background check before their start date. Employees working on the Northstar contract get an extra check." *(Analyst note: sampled 5 personnel files; all 5 had completed background checks. This control appears to be working.)*

**Q: Is there security awareness training?**
"We bought an online course in January. Everyone is supposed to finish it by April 30. I sent one reminder. I haven't chased the people who didn't finish. There's also a short module about handling CUI for Operations and Government Programs staff, but it's optional."

**Q: Do employees sign an acceptable use policy?**
"Yes, at hire. It's from 2021. I don't think anyone's looked at it since."

**Q: Physical security?**
"Everyone has a badge. The front door is locked, visitors sign in at the front desk and wear a visitor badge." *(Analyst note: observed badge reader and visitor log on site. Working as described.)*
