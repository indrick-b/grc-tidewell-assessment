# Interview Notes — Shauna Owens, Government Programs Manager (with Tom Alvarez, Director of Operations)

**Date:** October 29, 2026 · **Interviewer:** GRC Analyst · **Length:** 45 minutes
**Purpose:** Understand how CUI from the Northstar subcontract is received, stored, shared, and protected.

> Fictional interview for a practice assessment.

---

**Q: What kind of information do you get from Northstar?**
Shauna: "Shipping manifests for military equipment. Item lists, quantities, pickup and delivery locations, dates. They're stamped CUI at the top."

**Q: How do the files get to you?**
Shauna: "Northstar emails some of them. For big batches, their logistics lead shares a Dropbox folder, and we drop our updated manifests back into it. A few of us use our personal Dropbox accounts because the company doesn't have one."

**Q: Where are they stored at Tidewell?**
Tom: "On the GovPrograms share on FS01. Some people also keep copies on their laptops so they can work from home."

**Q: Do you know which laptops have CUI on them?**
Tom: "Not exactly. Anyone in Operations or Government Programs might."

**Q: Is there a System Security Plan for the Northstar contract?**
Shauna: "A what? We signed the subcontract in 2024. Northstar sent a letter last month asking for one, along with something called a SPRS score. I forwarded it to Kevin." *(Analyst note: see `01-evidence/requests/northstar-letter.md`.)*

**Q: Do you have a list of open security gaps you're working on for the contract?**
Shauna: "No."

**Q: Did the September email incident touch any CUI?**
Shauna: "I don't know. Dana in accounts payable gets our invoices for Northstar, and sometimes manifests are attached to those emails."

**Q: Did anyone tell Northstar about the incident?**
Shauna: "No. Nobody told me it was something we had to report."
