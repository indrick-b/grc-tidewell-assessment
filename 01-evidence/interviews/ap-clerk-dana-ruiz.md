# Interview Notes — Dana Ruiz, Accounts Payable Clerk

**Date:** October 31, 2026 · **Interviewer:** GRC Analyst · **Length:** 25 minutes
**Purpose:** Understand how payments and vendor bank changes work, and what happened in September from the user's side.

> Fictional interview for a practice assessment.

---

**Q: How do customers pay Tidewell?**
"ACH or wire transfer. We send our bank details on the invoice. We don't take credit cards. We stopped in 2020 because of the fees."

**Q: How do customers know the bank details are really from us?**
"They don't, really. It's on the invoice PDF. There's nothing on our website about it."

**Q: What happens when one of our vendors says their bank account changed?**
"They email me, and I update it in LedgerPoint. If it's a big vendor, I sometimes call them, but there's no rule that says I have to."

**Q: Who approves payments?**
"I enter the bill, and I can approve and release it myself if it's under $10,000. Over that, Priya approves. Most of our vendor payments are under $10,000."

**Q: Did anything unusual come in recently?**
"SecureShred emailed me in October saying they merged with another company and have a new bank. I updated it. Their next payment goes out in the middle of November."

**Q: Tell me about the September email.**
"It looked exactly like a Microsoft page saying my password was expiring. I typed it in. I didn't think about it again until the customer called. I felt terrible. Nobody ever showed me what a fake page looks like. I never finished the training. I kept meaning to."

**Q: Do you use MFA on LedgerPoint?**
"No. It's the same password as my email. Is that bad?"

**Q: Do you keep any Northstar manifests?**
"Northstar invoices come with the manifest attached as backup, so they're in my inbox. I don't open them much."
