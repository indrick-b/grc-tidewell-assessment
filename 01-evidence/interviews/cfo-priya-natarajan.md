# Interview Notes — Priya Natarajan, Chief Financial Officer

**Date:** October 30, 2026 · **Interviewer:** GRC Analyst · **Length:** 30 minutes
**Purpose:** Understand vendors, contracts, insurance, policies, and leadership priorities.

> Fictional interview for a practice assessment.

---

**Q: How do you choose and review vendors?**
"Price and references. We don't ask vendors security questions. Once the contract is signed, we don't review them again unless there's a billing problem."

**Q: Which vendors can see your most sensitive data?**
"PayNest runs payroll, so they have everyone's Social Security numbers and bank accounts. Bayside IT Partners has full access to our systems. RouteLine tracks our shipments, including the Northstar ones."

**Q: Have you ever asked a vendor for a SOC 2 report?**
"Microsoft has one, I think IT downloaded it once. I don't know about the others."

**Q: Who owns the company's security policies?**
"Nobody, really. Kevin's predecessor wrote most of them before he left in 2019. They've never been formally approved or reviewed."

**Q: Is there a risk register or any list of known risks?**
"No. That's part of why we hired you."

**Q: What does leadership care about most right now?**
"Three things, in this order. Keeping our insurance, because without it Brightwater will drop us. Keeping the Northstar contract, which is 30% of revenue. And not having another $48,500 mistake. We have some budget, but not unlimited. Tell us what gives us the most protection for the money."
