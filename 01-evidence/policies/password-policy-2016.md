## 1. Purpose
To make sure employees use strong passwords.

## 2. Scope
All employees.

## 3. Rules
1. Passwords must be at least **8 characters**.
2. Passwords must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.
3. Passwords must be changed every **90 days**.
4. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
5. Accounts lock after 10 failed login attempts.
6. Employees should not write down their passwords.
7. IT may set passwords to never expire for special accounts.

## 4. Enforcement
Employees who break this policy may be disciplined.
