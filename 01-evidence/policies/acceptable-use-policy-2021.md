## Key rules
1. Company devices and accounts are for business use.
2. **Company data must be stored only on company-approved systems.** Personal email and personal cloud storage (for example, personal Dropbox or Google Drive) may not be used for company data.
3. Employees must lock their computer when they walk away.
4. Employees must report lost or stolen devices to IT within 24 hours.
5. Employees must report suspicious emails to IT.
6. Every employee signs this policy at hire.
