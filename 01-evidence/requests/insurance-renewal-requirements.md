# Cyber Insurance Renewal Requirements — Harborline Mutual

> Fictional insurer for a practice assessment.

**Policyholder:** Tidewell Logistics, Inc.
**Renewal date:** January 15, 2027

To renew, the policyholder must confirm in writing that each control below is in place. Coverage may be denied if a claim results from a control the policyholder said was in place but was not.

| # | Required control |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | MFA on all email accounts |
| 2 | MFA on all remote access (VPN, remote desktop) |
| 3 | MFA on all administrator accounts |
| 4 | Endpoint detection and response (EDR) on all laptops and servers |
| 5 | Backups kept offline or in a separate location, and tested at least once a year |
| 6 | Security awareness training for all employees each year, including phishing |
| 7 | A written incident response plan, reviewed in the last 12 months |
| 8 | No internet-facing systems running unsupported operating systems |

*Note from the insurer:* The September 2026 funds-transfer loss is under review. A second similar claim within 24 months may not be covered.
