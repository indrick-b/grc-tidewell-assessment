# Letter from Northstar Defense Services (Prime Contractor)

> Fictional letter for a practice assessment.

**From:** Supplier Cybersecurity Office, Northstar Defense Services
**To:** Shauna Owens, Government Programs Manager, Tidewell Logistics
**Date:** October 6, 2026
**Subject:** Subcontractor cybersecurity requirements under Subcontract NDS-2024-117

Ms. Owens,

Your subcontract includes DFARS 252.204-7012, *Safeguarding Covered Defense Information and Cyber Incident Reporting*. As our subcontractor, Tidewell must protect Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) on its systems by implementing the 110 security requirements in NIST SP 800-171.

The contract also requires a current CMMC Level 2 self-assessment, with the score entered in the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS).

By **February 1, 2027**, please provide:

1. Confirmation that a current **System Security Plan (SSP)** exists for the systems that store, process, or send CUI.
2. A **Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M)** for any requirements not yet met.
3. Your current self-assessment score as entered in SPRS.
4. The name of the person responsible for cybersecurity on this subcontract.

As a reminder, DFARS 252.204-7012 requires cyber incidents affecting covered defense information to be reported to the Department within **72 hours** of discovery, and Tidewell must notify Northstar as well.

We value our partnership and want to help you meet these requirements.

Supplier Cybersecurity Office
Northstar Defense Services
