## Who Tidewell works for

Tidewell has to answer to **both** the private sector and the government.

**Government side.** Tidewell is a subcontractor to *Northstar Defense Services* (fictional), a prime contractor on a Department of War logistics contract. Tidewell plans shipments of military equipment and receives shipping manifests marked **CUI** (Controlled Unclassified Information). The subcontract includes DFARS clause 252.204-7012, which requires Tidewell to protect CUI using **NIST SP 800-171** and to report cyber incidents to the Department within 72 hours.

**Private side.** Tidewell's largest commercial customer, *Brightwater Retail* (fictional), has sent a security questionnaire and wants Tidewell to have a **SOC 2** report within 12 months. Tidewell's cyber insurance policy also renews soon, and the insurer has new minimum security requirements.
