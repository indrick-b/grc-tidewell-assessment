## 1. Purpose
*One or two sentences: why does this policy exist?*

## 2. Scope
*Who and what does it cover? Think about employees, contractors, vendors (Bayside IT), admin accounts, service accounts, email, VPN, and the systems that hold CUI.*

## 3. Password requirements
*Base these on NIST SP 800-63B-4, section 3.1.1.2. Cover the following:*
- *Minimum length when a password is the only factor, and when it's used with MFA*
- *Maximum length the system must allow*
- *Whether composition rules (forced symbols, numbers) are allowed*
- *When passwords must be changed (and when they must not be forced to change)*
- *Checking new passwords against a list of common and breached passwords*

## 4. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
*Who must use MFA, and for what? (Email, VPN, admin accounts, remote access.)*

## 5. Admin, shared, and service accounts
*Rules for admin accounts (named, separate from daily accounts), shared accounts (allowed or not?), and service accounts (who owns them, how their passwords are managed).*

## 6. Account lockout
*How many failed attempts before lockout, and how a user gets unlocked safely (identity verification at the help desk).*

## 7. Exceptions
*How someone requests an exception, who approves it, and how long it lasts.*
