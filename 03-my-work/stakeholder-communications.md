# One Risk, Three Audiences

> Practice document. A GRC analyst has to explain the same problem to very different people. Each audience cares about something different, so the words change and the facts don't.

| Audience | What they care about | What to lead with | What to leave out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IT Manager (technical) | What exactly to change, and how much work it is | The settings, accounts, and steps | Business jargon |
| CEO (business) | Money, contracts, customers, decisions | The impact and the decision you need | Control IDs and acronyms |
| Northstar (formal, outside) | Whether Tidewell meets the contract | Facts, dates, and the plan | Internal blame or speculation |

## Example: MFA (the first one is done for you; fill in the brackets from your own testing)

### To Kevin Doyle, IT Manager
> **Subject:** MFA rollout — accounts and plan
>
> Kevin, the M365 report shows [your count] enabled accounts with MFA off, including [list any admin accounts you found]. The VPN is password-only. Insurer deadline is January 15. Could you draft a rollout plan by November 20: admins and Bayside first, then Finance and Government Programs, then everyone else, with VPN in the same window? I'll write the user notice and help desk script so the call volume is manageable. Thanks.

### To Marcus Hale, CEO
*Your turn. Three to five sentences. No acronyms except MFA (explain it once). Lead with what's at stake and the decision you need.*

### To Northstar Defense Services (for the POA&M cover letter)
*Your turn. Formal. State the requirement (NIST SP 800-171 3.5.3), the current status honestly, and the POA&M item and date.*
